## First Comment (THE MOST IMPORTANT PART — post IMMEDIATELY after launching)

Hey ProductHunt! Oscar here, founder of restocking 👋

**The problem**: I was tired of refreshing Zara 15 times a day to catch a restock in my girlfriend's size. Every time I checked, it was already gone. The native "back in stock" emails arrive hours late (when they arrive at all).

**What we built**: A worker that continuously monitors 120+ European fashion retailers — checking every 5 minutes on Pro — and sends instant email/SMS alerts when a specific size comes back in stock. Not just "this product is back" but "YOUR size is back."

**How it's different**:
- 🇪🇺 European-first: Zara, COS, Aritzia, Sézane, Mango, Uniqlo... not just US/UK brands
- 👖 Size-specific: we track individual sizes, not just products
- ⚡ <5 min detection: continuous polling, not hourly batches
- 🎯 Double confirmation: 2 consecutive IN_STOCK checks before alerting (eliminates false positives)
- 🆓 Freemium: 3 alerts free, Pro at €5.99/month

**Tech stack**: Next.js 16, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Python workers on Railway, Resend for email, Twilio for SMS, DeepSeek for LLM-based product extraction, Stripe for payments. iOS app on the App Store (React Native/Expo).

**Why now**: The European fashion resale market is exploding (€33B by 2030), but the restock experience hasn't evolved since 2015. Most tools are US-focused browser extensions. We're building the cloud-native, mobile-first European alternative.

1. The onboarding flow — is it clear?

**Special launch offer**: First 100 signups get 3 months of Pro for free. No credit card required to start.
